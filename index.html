Percept Mark Zero

A kit-sized fixed-wing a non-pilot can run.

Loiter over an area, tap a person or animal on video to Follow, Cancel back to loiter, and keep a safe flight plan if the link drops. No sticks. No goggles. Confirm and cancel only.

Why

Most public-safety and search-and-rescue drones are quads flown by collateral Part 107 pilots. Agencies already own aircraft; the bottleneck is operators, training, and VLOS/BVLOS friction—not airframe count. Fixed wing covers more ground, but once you find someone there is no simple non-pilot way to hold or follow without a skilled pilot on the sticks.

What I’m building

  • Loiter → Select → Follow → Cancel / Return
  • Onboard vision track so the aircraft can keep the follow when ground video is bad
  • Stock ArduPlane + a thin operator UI
  • Observation / practice first: search, hold, watch

Who it’s for

Thin SAR teams, wildland agencies, and conservation groups that need longer endurance than a quad and can’t staff another remote pilot for every flight.

Status

Side project with a friend. Sim work in progress (Select / Follow / Cancel + link-loss behavior). Hardware on the bench.
